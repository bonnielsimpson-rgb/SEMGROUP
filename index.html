2. Group preferences

For each student, choose gender and whether a same-gender group is preferred or not important in the roster above. Same-gender preferences are optional and never prevent groups from being created. Students who prefer not to disclose gender can select no preference. Optional: choose two students and say whether they should be kept apart or preferably placed together. Time zones and approximate local-time availability are considered automatically; neither guarantees a meeting time. Exceptions are allowed.

3. Create groups

Create Groups: Make a set of groups using the roster and preferences.

Lock a group: Click 🔓 Lock group on any group you want to keep. Click again to unlock it.

Remix Unlocked Groups: Keep locked groups unchanged and rearrange everyone else. This does not save or change previous rounds.

Keep These Groups: Save the chosen arrangement to group history so future rounds can avoid repeating the same pairings.

4. Previous rounds

These are the group assignments you chose to keep. Future groups will try to pair different students together. Required “keep apart” rules always take priority.
